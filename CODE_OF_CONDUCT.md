# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue with the idea, not the person.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change without it being a judgement
  of you or your work.
- Keep criticism technical and specific. "This is wrong because X" is useful;
  "this is amateurish" is not.
- Respect the project's scope. It is a single-operator research tool, and
  declining feature requests is not hostility.

## What is not

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## A note specific to this project

This repository deals with investing, which attracts strong opinions and
occasional bad actors. Two things are firmly out of bounds:

- **Promotion.** Do not use issues, pull requests, or discussions to advertise
  a service, newsletter, token, fund, or course.
- **Advice.** Do not solicit personal investment advice, and do not offer it.
  This project deliberately produces no buy or sell recommendations, and its
  discussion areas are not a place to work around that.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits — and when representing the project elsewhere.

Report problems via [private vulnerability
reporting](https://github.com/umr240/investment-intelligence/security/advisories/new)
if the matter is sensitive, or by opening an issue if it is not. The maintainer
will review and respond as time allows, and may edit, hide, or delete
contributions, or block accounts, where this document is not respected.

This is a small project maintained by one person. Enforcement will be
proportionate and imperfect; the aim is a place where technical disagreement is
easy and unpleasantness is not.
